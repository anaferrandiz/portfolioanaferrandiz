After 10 years, I went back to Morocco — with some resistance and some doubts, but I went back. We visited places I had already been to and others I didn't know. Almost a month through Tangier, Assilah, Chefchaouen, Akchour, Fez, Meknes, Agadir, Essaouira and Marrakech. It coincided with Ramadan, which made the visit particularly interesting. We were grateful for the calm and the unhurried pace. Everything I photographed was on film: four rolls of Ultramax 400.
